Minecraft Clipper

Minecraft Clipper is a personal automation tool that cuts, captions, and posts short highlight clips from Minecraft gameplay recordings to the developer's own TikTok account, using TikTok's Content Posting API.

Privacy Policy

This application accesses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload video content chosen by the developer to their own TikTok account.

  • No personal data is collected from any third party or other user.
  • No data accessed via the TikTok API is shared, sold, or transferred to any other party.
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally and used only to authorize video uploads to the developer's own account.
  • Video content processed by this app (recordings, transcripts, captions) is stored locally on the developer's own device and is not transmitted anywhere other than to TikTok for the purpose of posting.
